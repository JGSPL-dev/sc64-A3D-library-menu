# Extras

Not needed to use the menu.

- `tools/A3D Pixel Test.z64`: test ROM showing 1-4px line patterns at 640 and 800 pixels wide (press A to switch).
  It showed that the Analogue 3D displays 640 across pixel-perfect and doesn't support wider framebuffers.
  `pixeltest-main.c` is its source.
- `tools/bake_sprites.py`: pre-renders the cartridge and 64DD art at exact on-screen sizes
  (also in the source, under tools/).
- `screens/`: simulated layouts drawn during development. They are mockups, not screen captures.
